## 1. Layer Chunky Knit Blankets

![A close-up of layered chunky knit blankets in cream and warm brown on a made bed with soft winter light](cozy-winter-bedroom-layered-blankets.svg)

Chunky knit blankets add instant warmth and texture to a winter bedroom. Drape one across the foot of the bed and fold another over the arm of a chair so the room looks inviting from the door. Choose cream, oatmeal or warm brown so the layers sit calmly together.

A second, lighter throw on top stops the bed looking heavy. Mix a waffle weave with a cable knit for contrast, and keep the palette to two or three warm neutrals so the bed stays restful rather than busy.

## 2. Add Warm Fairy Lights

![Warm white fairy lights draped along a wooden headboard above a bed with cream bedding and soft cushions](cozy-winter-bedroom-fairy-lights.svg)

Warm fairy lights give a bedroom a soft evening glow without the harshness of a ceiling light. Drape a single strand along the headboard or around a window frame and keep the rest of the lighting low so the room feels calm.

Battery fairy lights with a timer are the easiest option because there are no wires to hide. Choose warm white rather than cool white, and keep the strand loose and uneven so it looks relaxed instead of staged.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I make my bedroom feel cosy in winter?

Layer soft textures, warm light and a calm colour palette, then keep clutter low so the room feels restful. Start with the bed, add one warm light source, and finish with a scent you enjoy.

### What colours work best for a cosy winter bedroom?

Warm neutrals such as cream, oatmeal, soft brown and muted terracotta work well because they feel calm in low winter light. Add one deeper accent, such as forest green or burgundy, for contrast.
