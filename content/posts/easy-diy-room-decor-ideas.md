Easy DIY room decor ideas are the fastest way to make a room feel like yours. You do not need a big budget, new furniture, or any experience — just a free afternoon, a few simple materials, and one good idea to start with. This list has 32 easy DIY room decor ideas, each with a photo, from layered bedding and handmade cushions to floating shelves, macrame wall hangings, fairy lights, and hand-painted jars.

Every idea here is simple enough to finish in a day, and most of them cost very little. Pick the one that fits your room, gather what you need, and start small — one shelf, one corner, one wall. By the end of the weekend, your room will look and feel completely different.

Save this list for later, and try one idea at a time. The best rooms are not decorated in a day — they grow one handmade piece at a time.

## 1. Layered Blankets and Throws on the Bed

Start with the bed, because it is the biggest thing in the room. In this terracotta bedroom, a rust-orange duvet, a sage-green throw folded across the foot, and two patterned cushions do all the work — no paint, no new furniture, nothing drilled into the wall.

Layer two or three textures that feel different: a smooth cotton duvet, a knitted throw, and a woven cushion. Keep the colors in one warm family so the layers look planned, not piled up. Fold the throw in thirds across the foot of the bed and let one corner hang loose.

![Terracotta bedroom with layered orange bedding, an easy DIY room decor idea](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-layered-blankets-orange-bedroom.jpg)

## 2. A Handmade Headboard for the Bedroom

Four green damask fabric panels set into a pale wooden frame make a headboard that looks bought, not made. It is really just fabric, wadding, and a simple frame — an afternoon project that changes the whole wall behind the bed.

Cut the frame from pine battens, wrap each panel in wadding and your chosen fabric, and staple it neatly at the back. Hang it low, just above the pillows, and keep the bedding plain so the pattern can be the star of the room.

![Handmade fabric headboard with green damask panels above a cosy bed](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-handmade-fabric-headboard.jpg)

## 3. Embroidered Cushions for a Soft, Colorful Sofa

A plain sofa wakes up fast with embroidered cushions. Here, coral, teal, and soft pink floral covers sit together on a light sofa, with a crocheted throw and a leafy plant nearby — bright, but calm enough to live with every day.

You can embroider a plain cushion cover with a simple running stitch, or sew covers from leftover fabric and add one embroidered flower each. Mix two or three colors and keep one cushion plain so the pattern has room to breathe.

![Colourful embroidered cushions and a soft throw on a light sofa](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-soft-embroidered-cushions-sofa.jpg)

## 4. Warm Fairy Lights and a Leafy Garland

A string of warm fairy lights and a leafy garland turn a plain bedroom wall into the coziest corner of the house. In this room they run along the ceiling above the bed, with a small macrame hanging and bunting underneath.

Use LED fairy lights, which stay cool, and drape the garland first so the lights follow its line. Keep the rest of the bedding pale and simple — the glow does the decorating for you, especially in the evening.

![Bedroom wall decorated with warm fairy lights and a leafy garland](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-fairy-lights-garland-bedroom.jpg)

## 5. A Cozy Reading Corner by the Bookshelves

A reading corner only needs three things: somewhere soft to sit, good light, and books within reach. This one has a buttoned armchair, a tall floor lamp, and a whole wall of bookshelves, with a cup of tea waiting on a small wooden side table.

Push an armchair into the quietest corner you have, add a floor lamp behind it, and stack a few books on a stool or small table. A folded throw over the arm finishes it — and the corner is ready in an afternoon.

![Cosy reading corner with an armchair, floor lamp and full bookshelves](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-cozy-reading-corner-bookshelves.jpg)

## 6. Handmade Patchwork Cushion Covers

Patchwork cushion covers are the easiest way to use up fabric scraps, and they look completely at home on a white wooden sofa. These pink, cream, and patterned squares are sewn into simple covers, with a grey throw and a woven floor cushion nearby.

Cut your fabric into equal squares, sew them into rows, then into a cover with a plain back. You do not need perfect corners — small wobbles are part of the charm. Make two or three and mix them with one plain cushion.

![Handmade patchwork cushion covers in soft pink and cream on a sofa](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-handmade-patchwork-cushion-covers.jpg)

## 7. A Large Handmade Macrame Wall Hanging

One large macrame hanging can carry a whole wall. This cream piece hangs from a wooden dowel above a grey sofa, with long fringe, a diamond pattern in the middle, and plants on both sides to soften the edges.

Start with cotton cord and a dowel, and learn two knots — the square knot and the lark's head knot will carry you through most of the pattern. Work from the middle outwards, trim the fringe last, and hang it at eye level above the sofa.

![Large handmade macrame wall hanging above a grey sofa with plants](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-macrame-wall-hanging-sofa.jpg)

## 8. A Simple Handmade Centrepiece for the Table

A centrepiece does not need flowers from a shop. This one is a glass jar of pink and yellow roses, a tall white candle, a stack of old books, and a few apples and pears — everything already in the house, arranged in five minutes.

Start with one tall thing in the middle, like a jar of flowers or a candle, then add lower pieces around it: books, fruit, or small candles. Keep it low enough to see over at dinner, and change the flowers with the seasons.

![Handmade table centrepiece with pink roses, candles and stacked books](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-handmade-table-centrepiece-roses.jpg)

## 9. A Woven Basket for Shoes by the Door

Shoes by the door are the fastest mess in the house, and a woven basket is the fastest fix. This seagrass basket sits under the entryway console with a folded throw over the edge, holding shoes out of sight but still easy to grab.

Pick a basket wide enough for two pairs and low enough to slide under a table. Line it with an old towel if the shoes are muddy, and keep one pair out for guests — the basket does the tidying for you.

![Woven storage basket with shoes under an entryway console table](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-storage-basket-entryway-console.jpg)

## 10. A Small Entryway Hook Board

Five dark wooden boards with hooks turn a blank hallway wall into a place for coats, bags, and backpacks. Nothing here is fancy — just boards, hooks, and screws — but the hallway finally has a job to do.

Screw the boards into wall studs, spacing the hooks wide enough for a coat and a bag each. Paint them dark for contrast on a pale wall, or leave the wood bare for a softer look. Hang it at a height everyone in the house can reach.

![Dark wood entryway hook board holding coats, a bag and backpack](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-entryway-hook-board-coats.jpg)

## 11. A Marble Tray to Tidy the Entryway

A marble tray on the console table gives keys, a watch, and a candle one place to live. This white tray with a thin gold edge sits on a wooden console under a bold painting, and the whole entryway suddenly looks finished.

You can make one from a marble tile with small gold handles, or buy a plain tray and paint the edge gold. Keep only what you use every day on it — keys, sunglasses, and one candle — and everything else goes in a drawer.

![Marble tray with a candle and keys on an entryway console table](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-marble-tray-entryway-table.jpg)

## 12. Fairy Lights and a Heart Wreath over the Fireplace

A stone fireplace with a lit stove is already cozy, and a red heart wreath and a string of warm fairy lights along the mantel make it the heart of the room. A basket of logs, a woven lantern, and two floral armchairs finish the scene.

Make the wreath from dried flowers or fabric scraps on a wire ring, and hang it above the mantel with a single nail. Run LED fairy lights along the mantel edge, keeping them away from the heat, and let the fire do the rest.

![Stone fireplace decorated with a heart wreath and warm fairy lights](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-fairy-lights-fireplace-heart-wreath.jpg)

## 13. Bold Blue Floating Shelves

Painting one wall deep blue turns plain wooden shelves into a feature. Four long shelves hold books, plants, vases, and framed art, with a grey sofa and patterned cushions below — the blue makes every color on the shelves look richer.

Paint the wall first, then fix the shelves with hidden brackets so they seem to float. Style each shelf with one tall thing, one stack of books, and one plant, and leave gaps so the blue shows through between the pieces.

![Wooden floating shelves with books, plants and vases on a blue wall](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-floating-shelves-blue-wall.jpg)

## 14. Thick Wooden Floating Shelves

Thick wooden shelves feel solid and handmade, and they hold more than thin ones. These three deep shelves carry books, woven vases, framed prints, a lantern, and trailing eucalyptus, with a basket of books and a plant on the floor below.

Cut the shelves from reclaimed timber or thick pine, sand the edges smooth, and fix them with heavy-duty floating brackets into studs. Style the top shelf tallest, the middle shelf with frames, and the bottom shelf with one sculptural piece.

![Thick wooden floating shelves styled with books, plants and woven decor](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-thick-wooden-floating-shelves.jpg)

## 15. Grey Floating Shelves with Candles

Three grey wooden shelves on a plain wall make a calm little gallery: a trailing plant and framed print on top, two white candlesticks in the middle, and a small framed print with a potted plant at the bottom.

Stain pine boards grey, or paint them and sand the edges lightly for a weathered look. Keep the styling to two colors — white and green here — and leave the middle shelf half empty so the candles stand out.

![Grey floating shelves with candles, framed art and a trailing plant](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-grey-floating-shelves-candles.jpg)

## 16. A Simple Photo Display on the Wall

Framed family photos on staggered wooden shelves turn a blank wall above the sofa into something personal. The frames are mixed — white, wood, and one small print — with two eucalyptus vases and a trailing plant to soften the rows.

Print your favorite photos in two sizes, frame them simply, and lean them on the shelves instead of hanging them — you can swap them whenever you like. Add one plant per shelf and keep the frames to one color family so the photos, not the frames, do the talking.

![Framed family photos and small plants on floating shelves above a sofa](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-photo-display-floating-shelves.jpg)

## 17. Floating Shelves in the Hallway

A hallway wall is wasted space until you shelve it. Four long wooden ledges run along this one, holding lanterns, books, plants, and white vases, with a patterned rug below — the hallway now feels like a room of its own.

Fix the shelves at staggered heights so tall lanterns and plants fit, and keep the heaviest pieces low. Style each ledge with one lantern, one stack of books, and one plant, and leave the wall above the top shelf bare.

![Long floating shelves in a hallway with lanterns, books and plants](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-floating-shelves-hallway-lanterns.jpg)

## 18. A Trailing Plant Wall on Wooden Shelves

Three dark wooden shelves hold a small jungle: trailing pothos spilling over the edges, a white lamp, books, and a woven basket below. The plants do the decorating — the shelves are just there to hold them up.

Start with two or three trailing plants in simple pots, and let them grow down over the shelf edge. Water them in place with a small jug, and trim the trails when they reach the shelf below. One shelf of plants beats a whole wall of ornaments.

![Wooden wall shelves filled with trailing plants, books and a lamp](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-wall-shelf-trailing-plants.jpg)

## 19. Wooden Cube Shelves with a Candle

Three wooden cube shelves on a sage-green wall hold almost nothing — a lit candle, a few books, a spotted mug, and a tiny basket — and that is the point. The empty space makes each piece look considered, not cluttered.

Build the cubes from thin pine boards, or buy plain ones and stain them to match. Hang them at different heights so the eye moves across the wall, and put only one or two things in each cube. A single candle is enough to light the whole arrangement.

![Wooden cube shelves with a candle, books and a small basket](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-wooden-cube-shelves-candle.jpg)

## 20. An Armchair and Arc Lamp by the Bookshelf

A light-wood bookshelf, a soft armchair, and a black arc lamp make a reading spot that looks straight out of a bookshop. The shelves hold books in calm rows, with a plant and a vase to break the lines, and a throw over the chair arm.

Push the bookshelf against the quietest wall, angle the arc lamp over the chair, and keep a folded throw on the arm. Sort the books by size, not color, and leave one shelf half empty — a bookshelf with breathing room looks twice as good.

![Armchair and arc lamp beside a light wood bookshelf with books](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-bookshelf-armchair-reading-lamp.jpg)

## 21. A Quiet Reading Corner with Tea and a Throw

This reading corner has everything within arm's reach: a wingback chair with a green checked cushion, a knitted throw, a floor lamp, and a small round table holding an open book, a cup of tea, and a few biscuits.

You need only a comfortable chair, a warm lamp, and a table small enough to fit beside it. Add a throw and a cushion in one soft color, and keep the books you are actually reading on the table — the corner will get used every day.

![Quiet reading corner with an armchair, open book and warm lamp](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-reading-corner-tea-throw.jpg)

## 22. A Beaded Macrame Wall Hanging in Warm Browns

A half-circle macrame hanging in browns, cream, and rust, with wooden beads along the top curve, is the whole decoration on this grey wall. It is small, simple, and handmade — and it warms up a plain wall on its own.

Knot the piece on a wooden dowel, working a half-circle shape with two or three knot patterns and a row of beads near the top. Trim the fringe into a curve last, and hang it with a simple cord at eye level.

![Handmade macrame wall hanging in brown and cream, an easy DIY room decor idea](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-macrame-wall-hanging-brown-cream.jpg)

## 23. Handmade Macrame Star Hangings

Three pink macrame stars with beads, bows, and long tassels hang from a branch over a soft pink bed, with fairy lights woven through the leaves above. They are the kind of handmade piece that makes a bedroom feel like a storybook.

Make each star on a wire frame, knotting cotton cord in a mandala pattern and adding beads as you go. Tie a velvet bow and a beaded tassel to the bottom of each one, and hang them at different heights from a branch or a dowel.

![Handmade macrame star hangings with beads and fairy lights in a bedroom](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-macrame-star-hangings-fairy-lights.jpg)

## 24. A Wall of Small Handmade Macrame Hangings

Nine small macrame hangings in two rows turn a plain wall above a wooden sofa into a gallery. Each piece is a little different — diamonds, Vs, and tassels — but the same cream cord ties them together.

Make the pieces one at a time, keeping them all the same width but changing the pattern in each. Hang them in two straight rows with small nails, leaving a hand's width between them, and keep the sofa cushions plain so the wall can shine.

![Wall of small handmade macrame hangings above a wooden sofa](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-macrame-wall-hanging-gallery.jpg)

## 25. Framed Abstract Art above a Pink Sideboard

A big framed abstract print in pink, cream, and gold hangs above a pink cane sideboard, with a tall vase of pampas grass on top. The art does the decorating — the sideboard just gives it something to sit above.

You can paint the abstract yourself with two or three colors on a large canvas, or print a favorite pattern and frame it simply. Paint the sideboard to match one color in the art, and add one tall vase — nothing else is needed.

![Framed abstract art above a pink cane sideboard with a pampas vase](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-framed-art-pink-sideboard.jpg)

## 26. A Handmade Lampshade for Warm Light

A crocheted lampshade turns a plain bedside lamp into the warmest light in the room. The open lace lets the glow through in a soft pattern, with a ball of yarn, a half-finished piece, and a crochet hook on the table beside it.

Crochet the shade in the round to fit your lamp frame, using cotton yarn and an open stitch so the light shines through. Slip it over the frame, tie it at the top, and use a warm LED bulb so the yarn stays cool.

![Handmade crochet lampshade glowing warmly on a wooden bedside table](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-handmade-crochet-lampshade.jpg)

## 27. A Handmade Candle Holder in a Jar

A mint-green jar with a lace band and a twine bow holds a lit candle, with eucalyptus leaves scattered around it on the table. It is a five-minute make from a clean jam jar — and it smells as good as it looks.

Paint the outside of a clean jar with mint glass paint, tie twine in a bow around the neck, and drop in a tea light or a small pillar candle. Set it on a wooden board with a few leaves, and never leave it burning unattended.

![Mint green jar candle tied with twine, an easy DIY room decor idea](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-mint-jar-candle-twine.jpg)

## 28. Decorated Glass Jars with Candles

A whole table of decorated jars — lace bands, twine bows, greenery, and candles — looks like a craft fair display, but every piece started as an empty jar. Paintbrushes, twine, and a few white baubles sit beside them, ready for the next one.

Save your jars, wash off the labels, and decorate each one differently: lace, twine, paint, or dried flowers. Group them in threes on a wooden board with a candle in the middle — a set always looks better than a single jar.

![Decorated glass jars with candles, twine and greenery on a table](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-decorated-glass-jars-candles.jpg)

## 29. Warm Light in Glass Jar Lanterns

Three glass jar lanterns with wire handles stand on round wood slices, each holding a glowing candle. They are simple, sturdy, and made from jars that would otherwise be thrown away.

Wrap wire around the neck of a clean jar to make a handle, add a little sand or salt at the bottom for the candle to sit in, and hang them from a hook or set them on a wood slice. They are perfect for evenings on a table or a shelf.

![Glass jar lanterns with candles standing on round wood slices](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-glass-jar-lanterns-candles.jpg)

## 30. A Hand-Painted Floral Glass Jar

Pink and yellow flowers, green leaves, and two butterflies are painted straight onto a big glass jar, standing in bright sunlight by the plants. It is a vase, a decoration, and a little piece of art all at once.

Paint with acrylics made for glass, starting with the stems and leaves and adding the flowers last. Let each layer dry before the next, and seal it with a clear coat. Fill it with water and fresh stems, or leave it empty as a sunny decoration.

![Hand-painted floral glass jar vase in bright sunlight by plants](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-hand-painted-floral-glass-jar.jpg)

## 31. Hand-Painted Floral Vases with Daisies

Three glass vases painted with yellow and pink flowers hold fresh white daisies, with a little wooden tag tied to the middle one. They look like a florist's display, but the flowers are painted on — only the daisies are real.

Paint simple five-petal flowers in two colors on clean glass, add tiny white dots between them, and seal with a clear coat. Tie twine around the neck with a small tag, and fill with fresh daisies from the garden.

![Hand-painted floral vases holding fresh white daisies on a shelf](/images/easy-diy-room-decor-ideas/easy-diy-room-decor-hand-painted-vases-daisies.jpg)

## 32. Hand-Painted Sunflower Jars with Fresh Flowers

Sunflowers and small blue flowers painted on glass jars glow against fresh flowers behind them — white daisies, pink peonies, and purple freesias. The painted jars and the real flowers share the same colors, so the whole table feels like one arrangement.

Paint big sunflowers first, then fill the gaps with small blue and white flowers and green leaves. Keep the painting loose — wobbly petals look handmade, which is the point. Add a twine bow and fresh flowers, and the jars are ready for the table.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the easiest DIY room decor ideas for beginners?

Start with soft textiles and light. Layer a throw over the bed, swap in handmade cushion covers, and hang fairy lights or a simple macrame piece. These easy DIY room decor ideas need no tools, no paint, and no experience — just fabric, cord, and a free afternoon. Pick one corner, finish it, and build from there.

### How can I decorate my room without spending a lot of money?

Use what you already have. Paint old glass jars, tie twine around candles, rearrange books on handmade shelves, and sew cushion covers from fabric scraps. Thrift shops and the dollar store cover the rest. Easy DIY room decor ideas work best when you spend little and make much — a ten-dollar project can change a whole corner.

### How do I make a small room look cozy with DIY decor?

Add layers and warm light. A folded throw, soft cushions, a small rug, and one warm lamp can make a small room feel calm and lived-in. Keep the colors warm and the shelves tidy, and leave one surface clear so the room can breathe. Cozy comes from texture and light, not from filling every corner.

### What is the easiest wall decor I can make myself?

A macrame wall hanging or a row of floating shelves. Macrame needs only cotton cord, a wooden dowel, and a few basic knots you can learn in an afternoon. Shelves need two brackets, a board, and paint. Both fill a blank wall fast, and both look handmade in the best way — a little imperfect, full of character.

### How do I style floating shelves so they look tidy, not cluttered?

Give every shelf one job and some empty space. Mix books, one plant, and one handmade piece per shelf, and keep the colors to two or three. Step back after each shelf and remove one thing — tidy shelves are styled by taking away, not by adding more. Leave gaps so each piece can be seen.

### Are fairy lights safe to use in a bedroom?

Yes, if you use LED fairy lights, which stay cool to the touch. Keep them away from bedding, curtains, and paper, never leave them on overnight or when you leave the house, and check the wire for damage before each use. Battery lights are the safest choice for garlands and headboards.

### How do I make a reading corner in a small room?

You need three things: a comfortable chair, a light, and a place for a book and a cup. Push an armchair into a quiet corner, add a floor lamp or a warm table lamp, and keep a small side table within reach. A throw and one cushion finish it — the corner can fit in less than a square metre.

### What paint should I use for hand-painted glass jars and vases?

Use acrylic paint made for glass, or multi-surface acrylic with a clear sealant on top. Clean the glass with soap and water first, let it dry fully, and paint thin layers so the color stays smooth. Once sealed, hand-wash the jars gently — never put painted glass in the dishwasher.
